# Research methodology

The curriculum balances foundational patterns, common interview variations, and a smaller advanced set. It is not a measured ranking of company-specific question frequency.

Sources consulted during implementation:

- [LeetCode Top Interview 150](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/top-interview-150/)
- [LeetCode 75](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/leetcode-75/)
- [Grind 75](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/) and its [FAQ](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/faq)
- [Algorithm study cheatsheet](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/study-cheatsheet/)
- [Coding interview techniques](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/)

The useful recurring guidance is to learn patterns, clarify assumptions, explain the invariant, dry-run examples and edge cases, and communicate complexity. RecallCode turns these into question-specific flashcards rather than asking learners to memorize an entire answer.

Every study guide includes original context and examples, a solution explanation, time/space costs, a failure mode to watch for, a follow-up and complete Java code. Source pages are linked for the authoritative platform statement. No premium editorial or bulk scraped statement is included.

Detailed evidence boundaries and actually inspected pages:

- [Arrays/search/greedy](research-arrays.md)
- [Linked structures/trees/heaps](research-structures.md)
- [Graphs/backtracking/DP/bits](research-graph-dp.md)

Use the app after attempting the problem: explain the approach aloud, implement it without looking, test boundary cases, then use cards to retain the key decisions. Rating a card is self-report and is not evidence of coding mastery. Revisit the source and solve fresh variations.

All 250 implementations compile under Java 17 compatibility and run at least two authored cases. These are examples/edge cases, not exhaustive or official judge verification. Live page inspection was representative; additional catalog source URLs are references, not claims that every page was separately inspected.
